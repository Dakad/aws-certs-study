# Revision workflow

Use [LEARNING_NOTES.md](LEARNING_NOTES.md) as source material for targeted review, not as a card deck, scorecard, or task queue. [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) remains the source of truth for demonstrated evidence and phase status.

## When to use it

- Start before the first Phase 6 mixed practice set on October 16, 2026.
- Repeat immediately before each later timed mixed practice set.
- Spend at most 10–15 minutes on revision before starting the set.
- Do not add a daily review schedule. After two mixed-practice cycles, assess whether this workflow improves recall without becoming repetitive.

## Suggested shortlist

Before a mixed set, read `LEARNING_NOTES.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, and relevant domain notes. Suggest 3–5 candidates in chat. Each candidate states:

- The concept or operational decision to revise.
- Its source note and date.
- A proposed format: concise reminder, flashcard-style recall, or operational scenario.
- A changed condition that tests transfer rather than repeating the prior rule.
- Why it was selected.

Build a balanced shortlist from:

- A current phase gap.
- An older unresolved correction.
- An item that improves exam-weighted coverage.

The learner chooses, skips, or defers candidates. Do not create reusable quiz content merely because a candidate was suggested.

## Guardrails

- Do not convert every note into a prompt or add per-note tags, scores, or review state.
- Do not repeat a prompt unless it remains unresolved, appears in a missed practice question, or is tested through a materially different scenario.
- Use original scenarios. Preserve the distinction between a guided answer, independent recall, and executed lab evidence.
- Update `PROGRESS.md` only from observed evidence. Append personal context to `LEARNING_NOTES.md` in date order.

## Reusable content

Create a scenario under `aws-cloudops/knowledge/scenarios/` only after a selected candidate proves useful and can be written as a complete, original operational question. Follow the scenario template and quiz-authoring guidance at that time.
